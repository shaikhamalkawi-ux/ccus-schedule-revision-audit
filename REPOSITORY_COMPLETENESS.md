# Repository completeness

See [Reproducibility scope](docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_SCOPE.md) for the authoritative public-access boundary and [Input contract](docs/INPUT_AND_OUTPUTS.md) for execution requirements.

The public snapshot supports synthetic execution, aggregate consistency checks and recomputation of the documented 2025–2026 calculation when a lawful pre-admitted cohort is supplied. It does not independently establish raw-source identity matching, reproduce all 2024–2026 analyses, train the exploratory classifier, or implement the proposed fuzzy future work.

Do not infer complete raw-source reproducibility from a passing synthetic demonstration or an aggregate arithmetic check. Original workbooks, record-level cohorts and private model objects are excluded from the curated archive.
